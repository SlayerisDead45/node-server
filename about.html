About Us

CleanFresh has been providing top quality laundry services for over 2 years. We started small but now serve 240+ happy customers every month.

Our team works hard to make sure your clothes come back clean, fresh and neatly folded. We handle everything from regular wash and fold to dry cleaning and blanket washing.

We believe clean clothes should be easy and affordable for everyone. That is why we keep our prices simple and our service fast.
